GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Is ARDENT currently open?

ARDENT is a developing facility concept. The spaces, services and programs shown here communicate the vision and are not represented as currently open or available.

Can I apply to become a tenant?

Yes. You can submit an expression of interest through the member portal. Applications are for planning and review; no lease or space is currently being offered through this website.

Can I book a studio or enroll in a class today?

You can submit an interest or preliminary request. Availability, schedules, pricing and confirmed bookings will be announced when operations are established.

How can my organization support ARDENT?

Use the inquiry form to express interest in sponsorship, partnerships, community programming or other ways to contribute.
