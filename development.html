ARDENT · DEVELOPMENT VISION · CENTRAL FLORIDA

A creative campus.
A destination for possibility.

ARDENT is envisioned as a multi-disciplinary creative arts complex bringing creators, educators, audiences and independent businesses together in one connected environment.

Explore the concept

THE BIG IDEA

Designed as an ecosystem,
not just a collection of rooms.

The long-term vision is a regional destination where people can develop skills, create professional work, share experiences, build creative businesses and connect with opportunity. The final site, size, mix of uses, costs and schedule will be determined through feasibility, planning, financing and community input.

01 / CREATE

Production & creation

Music rehearsal, recording and production, podcasting, film and soundstage, green screen, photography, dance and visual arts.

02 / LEARN

Education & development

Rock School, master classes, after-school programs, accessible arts learning, mentorship and online education.

03 / EXPERIENCE

Performance & culture

Live music, concert and performance spaces, gallery exhibitions, showcases, community events and creative gatherings.

04 / BUILD

Creative enterprise

Business support, coworking, artist services and opportunities for carefully selected independent creative operators.

05 / CONNECT

Community & access

Welcoming pathways for artists, students, families, visitors, educators, sponsors and local organizations.

06 / SHARE

Digital reach

Artist stories, internet radio concepts, online classes, media content and opportunities to connect beyond the physical campus.

A MIXED OPERATING MODEL

One destination.
Complementary operators.

The proposed model combines ARDENT-operated programs and signature experiences with independent businesses that add specialist services and activity to the campus. The structure is a planning concept, not a current tenant or lease offering.

ARDENT-OPERATED

Signature experiences

Potentially includes education, artist development, selected studios and production services, events, community initiatives, radio and shared facility operations.

INDEPENDENT TENANTS

Specialist businesses

Potentially includes instrument repair, photography, production companies, creative agencies, music retail and food or beverage operators, subject to site planning and approvals.

All uses are proposed possibilities. Final tenant mix, ownership, operating agreements, licensing, zoning and building requirements remain subject to professional review and approvals.
